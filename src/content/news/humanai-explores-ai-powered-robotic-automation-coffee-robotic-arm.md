Under the guidance of Dr. Chathuranga Hettiarachchi, HumanAI Research Lab is advancing research in intelligent robotic systems through the development of a robotic arm designed for automated coffee preparation, cleaning, and creative latte art applications.

![Robotic arm automation](https://snipboard.io/TABRn2.jpg)

The research focuses on integrating Artificial Intelligence, robotics, and automation to develop a versatile robotic platform capable of performing complex real-world tasks. The robotic arm is being explored for applications such as operating a coffee machine, carrying out cleaning activities, and executing precise robotic movements to create artistic latte designs.

![Coffee-making robotic arm in action](https://snipboard.io/ThHQAb.jpg)

By combining intelligent control, robotic precision, and human-centered interaction, this initiative aims to explore the potential of AI-driven robotic systems in everyday environments. This research highlights HumanAI Research Lab's commitment to interdisciplinary innovation and the development of emerging technologies that enhance human experiences through intelligent automation.
