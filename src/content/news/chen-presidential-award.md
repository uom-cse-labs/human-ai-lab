The Presidential Commission on Science & Technology awarded Dr. Chen for her groundbreaking "HITL Healthcare-Audit Protocol". The framework is currently deployed in regional rural clinics to monitor diagnostics equality, protecting patient data sovereignty while ensuring fair algorithmically-driven vaccine distribution.

## About the Award

The Presidential Award for Excellence in Science & Technology recognizes outstanding contributions by Sri Lankan researchers whose work demonstrates direct societal impact. Dr. Chen was nominated by the Ministry of Health for her work on algorithmic fairness in public health infrastructure.

## The HITL Protocol

The Human-in-the-Loop Healthcare-Audit Protocol introduces three key innovations:

1. **Continuous Audit Loops**: Real-time monitoring of diagnostic AI recommendations against patient outcomes
2. **Patient Data Sovereignty Layer**: Cryptographic consent management ensuring patients retain control over their data
3. **Equality Metrics Dashboard**: Population-stratified outcome analysis to detect algorithmic bias across demographic groups

## Impact

Since deployment across twelve rural clinics, the protocol has identified and corrected four distinct algorithmic bias patterns in diagnostic recommendation systems, improving equitable treatment outcomes by approximately 28%.
