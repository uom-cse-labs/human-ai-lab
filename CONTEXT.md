# Human-AI Lab

Website for the Human-AI Lab at University of Moratuwa. The lab studies and advances human-centered artificial intelligence.

## Language

**Human-AI Lab**:
A research lab at University of Moratuwa focused on human-centered AI research, education, and technology.
_Avoid_: HumanAI Lab, Human AI Lab

**Department of Computer Science and Engineering**:
The academic department within University of Moratuwa that hosts the Human-AI Lab.
_Avoid_: CSE, CS Department

**General Public Audience**:
The website targets all visitors — prospective students, researchers, industry partners, media, and the general public — with a clear overview of the lab's identity, mission, and work.

**Research Publication**:
A peer-reviewed paper, article, or preprint produced by lab members.
_Avoid_: Paper (too ambiguous), Article (too generic)

**Physical Lab Equipment**:
Hardware, apparatus, or infrastructure available in the lab's physical space.
_Avoid_: Facilities, gear

**Lab Member**:
A person affiliated with the Human-AI Lab — faculty, researcher, PhD student, MSc student, or undergraduate research assistant.
_Avoid_: People (too vague), Team (informal)

**Research Theme**:
A broad area of human-centered AI that the lab investigates. Specific themes are not yet defined — the website must accommodate future addition of research areas.
_Avoid_: Research domain, focus area

**Mission Statement**:
A concise declaration of the lab's purpose and focus. Not yet written — the website must include a slot for it when established.

**Lab Director**:
The senior academic who leads the Human-AI Lab. Currently: Eng. Prof. Indika Perera, PhD (St Andrews), CEng.
_Avoid_: PI, Head, Lead

**Lab Role**:
A position a person holds within the lab — e.g. Lab Director, Research Assistant. Specific member roster is to be determined.

**Lab Space**:
The physical location of the Human-AI Lab within the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Not yet established.
_Avoid_: Facilities, premises

**Website Scope — Informational**:
The website is purely informational / brochure-style. It communicates who the lab is, what it does, its members, publications, and equipment. No interactive features (portals, bookings, applications) are planned.

**Lab Logo**:
The visual mark of the Human-AI Lab. Not yet created — the website must accommodate a logo image when available.

**Lab Founding**:
The establishment date and origin story of the Human-AI Lab. Not yet determined.

**Partnership**:
An industry or academic collaboration the lab participates in. Not yet established — planned for the future.
_Avoid_: Collaboration (too generic)

**News Item**:
An announcement or update published on the website — e.g. new publication, event, partnership milestone. Sourced from Markdown files (static approach).
_Avoid_: Blog post, article (reserved for research publications)

**Lab Contact**:
Contact information for the Human-AI Lab. Not yet determined.

**University Affiliation**:
The website is hosted and managed under the University of Moratuwa, Department of Computer Science and Engineering. The site must display the university's branding and affiliation clearly.
_Avoid_: Parent institution, host university
