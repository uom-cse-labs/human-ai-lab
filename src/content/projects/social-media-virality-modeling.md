This project maps two research directions that are usually studied separately — how content spreads on social media, and what platform recommendation algorithms actually do — and brings them together under one framework.

The "forward" side covers how virality happens: diffusion and epidemic style models, structural and emotional drivers of spread, ML/GNN cascade prediction, and LLM-based multi-agent social simulation. The "inverse" side covers how platform algorithms can be inferred from the outside: feedback loop studies, platform audits, and model extraction/privacy attacks.

The project identifies a key gap — LLM agent simulation has never been used as an active tool to reverse engineer platform behavior. To address this, an LLM-based solution was developed as part of a five-part framework: a validated agent population, controlled probe design, live interaction with the platform, surrogate model inference, and audit style validation — as a path toward closing that gap.
