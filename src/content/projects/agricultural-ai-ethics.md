## Overview

Agricultural AI systems deployed in the Global South often rely on models trained on datasets from temperate regions, leading to systematic prediction errors when applied to tropical farming systems. This project develops bias audit methodologies and locally-calibrated models for smallholder farmers.

### Research Questions

- How do climate zone mismatches between training and deployment data affect prediction accuracy?
- Can transfer learning techniques effectively adapt temperate-region models to tropical contexts?
- What governance mechanisms are needed to ensure fair access to AI-driven agricultural advice?

## Field Studies

Field validation is being conducted across three agro-ecological zones in Sri Lanka, working directly with smallholder farming communities to understand their decision-making contexts and information needs.

## Current Status

Data collection phase is underway with 200 participating farms. Preliminary results indicate that uncalibrated models show up to 40% error in yield predictions for tropical mixed-cropping systems.
