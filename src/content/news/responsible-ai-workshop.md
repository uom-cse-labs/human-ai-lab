The event hosted 120 policy makers, technologists, and researchers at the Moratuwa University Auditorium. Keynote discussions centered around creating flexible regional standards for AI deployment, auditing bias in agricultural automation tools, and coordinating public-sector data standards across SAARC nations.

## Keynote Highlights

The opening address by Prof. Indika Perera emphasized the need for culturally-grounded AI governance frameworks that account for the unique socio-economic landscape of South Asia.

### Panel Discussions

Three concurrent panel tracks explored:

- **Regulatory Standards**: Comparative analysis of EU AI Act, India's AI principles, and Sri Lanka's emerging digital policy landscape
- **Agricultural AI Ethics**: Auditing bias in crop-yield prediction models trained on non-localized datasets
- **Public Data Sovereignty**: Cross-border data sharing standards for SAARC public health systems

## Outcomes

The workshop concluded with a joint communiqué outlining five priority action areas for responsible AI deployment in the region, including a commitment to establish a Southeast Asian AI Ethics Working Group.
