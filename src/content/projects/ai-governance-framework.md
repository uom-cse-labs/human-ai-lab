## Overview

This project develops a comprehensive AI governance framework tailored to the socio-economic landscape of Southeast Asia. Unlike one-size-fits-all approaches imported from Western regulatory contexts, our framework accounts for the region's unique cultural values, data sovereignty concerns, and developmental priorities.

### Key Objectives

- Establish region-specific AI ethics guidelines that respect local cultural norms
- Create audit protocols for bias detection in agricultural and healthcare AI systems
- Design cross-border data sharing standards for SAARC public health systems

## Methodology

The framework was developed through a series of participatory workshops with 120 policy makers, technologists, and researchers across six countries. The Human-in-the-Loop methodology ensures that governance recommendations balance technical feasibility with cultural acceptability.

## Expected Impact

The framework is expected to influence national AI policies across the region, with pilot implementations planned in Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, and Nepal by Q1 2025.
