## Overview

The HITL Healthcare-Audit Protocol introduces three key innovations for monitoring diagnostic AI systems in resource-constrained settings. Currently deployed across twelve rural clinics, the framework ensures algorithmic fairness while protecting patient data sovereignty.

### Core Components

1. **Continuous Audit Loops**: Real-time monitoring of diagnostic AI recommendations against patient outcomes
2. **Patient Data Sovereignty Layer**: Cryptographic consent management ensuring patients retain control over their data
3. **Equality Metrics Dashboard**: Population-stratified outcome analysis to detect algorithmic bias across demographic groups

## Results

Since deployment, the protocol has identified and corrected four distinct algorithmic bias patterns in diagnostic recommendation systems, improving equitable treatment outcomes by approximately 28%.
