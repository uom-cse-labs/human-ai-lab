The Department of Computer Science and Engineering welcomed the CSE 2025 batch with an insightful session on "Human-Centered AI and Future AI Applications" conducted by Eng. Prof. Indika Perera on Tuesday, 18th August.

![CSE 2025 Batch Session on Human-Centered AI](https://snipboard.io/F7Php1.jpg)

The session introduced students to the evolving landscape of Artificial Intelligence and highlighted the importance of designing AI systems that are aligned with human needs, experiences, and interactions. Eng. Prof. Indika Perera discussed how User Interfaces (UI) and User Experience (UX)-driven application design will be key factors influencing the widespread adoption and success of AI applications at a global scale.

Through this session, students gained valuable perspectives on the opportunities, challenges, and future directions in AI-driven application development, while understanding the role of computer engineers in creating innovative and human-centered technological solutions.

This initiative reflects the Department of Computer Science and Engineering's commitment to inspiring and preparing the next generation of engineers for emerging technologies and future advancements in AI.
