Millions of people struggle with stress, anxiety, and depression, but doctors mostly rely on occasional self-reports to check on mental health. This project looks at whether the way people use their smartphones — including screen time, app usage, sleep patterns, movement, and how often they talk to others — can reveal signs of stress, anxiety, or depression.

We reviewed existing research and datasets to understand which phone usage patterns are most linked to mental health, and whether simple machine learning models or more complex deep learning models work better for this kind of data.

Based on this review, we found that simple, explainable machine learning models work better than deep learning for phone usage data, especially when predicting for new users. The long-term goal is to build a mobile app that can help people monitor their mental health early.
